# Portfolio Shorts

Short, optional articles for students in the Portfolio courses at Noroff: Portfolio 1 (end of Year 1) and Portfolio 2 (end of Year 2).

These articles are published as Friday Knowledge Share articles during each course. Each one complements a lesson from Moodle, using different examples and coming at the topic from a slightly different angle.

> [!NOTE]
> These articles are extra reading for your own benefit. They are not part of the course material you are assessed on, and they never replace the Course Assignment Brief. If anything here seems to disagree with the brief, the brief wins.
